Garbage Disposal Installation Costs for Eagle, ID Homes

Written by Eagle Express Plumbing. Published October 15, 2026.

Garbage disposal installation costs depend on four things: the unit you pick, how it connects to power, what the sink drain looks like, and whether a dishwasher hooks into it. A swap of like for like is a short job, and a first-time install under a sink with no outlet is a longer one. We do not print dollar figures here because Eagle, ID prices are set on the job, but this page shows what builds a quote.

The short answer on what moves the price

Three decisions swing the bill more than anything else. They are the motor size, the electrical setup, and how much work the sink drain needs before the new unit fits. Labor time follows from those choices.

  • Motor horsepower and how much grinding power you want.
  • Plug-in cord or a hardwired connection.
  • A switch and outlet already present under or near the sink.
  • A dishwasher drain hose that must connect to the unit.
  • Drain pipe alignment with the existing trap and tailpiece.

If a quote lists only one lump sum, ask for the pieces. It is easier to compare two quotes when both show unit, labor, parts and disposal of the old unit.

Horsepower: what you are paying for

Disposal motors run from small to large, commonly sold in sizes from a third of a horsepower up to a full horsepower. A bigger motor spins faster, jams less and usually costs more. It is also louder in some models and heavier on the sink mounting.

A small household that rinses plates and scrapes soft scraps may be fine with a mid-size unit. A busy kitchen that cooks every night may want more power. Match the motor to the kitchen, since a bigger unit does not fix a sink that is slow because the drain is partly blocked.

Features add cost too. Sound insulation, stainless grinding parts, and batch-feed models that run only with the lid in place all raise the price of the unit before labor.

Plug-in versus hardwired

Many disposals come ready for a cord that plugs into an outlet under the sink, and some are wired directly into the home's circuit. The choice changes the job, because a cord needs a working outlet and a hardwired unit needs a wiring connection.

If there is already a switch and an outlet, the install is quicker. If the kitchen has neither, someone must add them, and that is electrical work. We handle the plumbing side, and a licensed electrician is the right call for new circuits or wall switch wiring.

The dishwasher knockout plug

Most disposals have a small plug inside the dishwasher inlet. If the dishwasher drains through the disposal, that plug must be knocked out before the hose is attached. Forget it, and the dishwasher cannot empty.

The plug has to come out of the unit before the hose is clamped on, and the pieces must be removed from inside the disposal. Leave one in, and it can jam the blades. A plumber checks this on the way, which is why a swap should include a test of the dishwasher cycle.

If a quote does not mention the dishwasher hose, ask. It is a small task, but it is the one people forget to price.

Drain fit, old unit removal and surprises

The new unit must line up with the existing drain pipe. If the old one sat at a different height, the trap arm may need cutting, new pieces, or a new tailpiece. Corroded or cracked fittings also show up once the old unit comes out.

Removal and haul-away of the old unit is usually part of the labor. Ask if it is included. A sink that has been dripping for months can also have a damaged cabinet floor, which is a repair outside the install.

If the sink itself drains slowly, a new disposal will not fix it. Our drain cleaning work clears the line, and the right order is to clear the drain first, then install.

Reading a disposal quote line by line

A fair comparison needs the same scope on both sheets. Put the items next to each other and mark what is missing.

  1. The brand, model and horsepower of the unit.
  2. Mounting hardware and the sink flange seal.
  3. Power connection: cord, hardwire, or a note that electrical work is separate.
  4. Dishwasher hose connection and knockout plug removal.
  5. Drain parts, like a new trap arm or tailpiece.
  6. Removal and disposal of the old unit.
  7. A test run for leaks and for the dishwasher drain.
  8. Who is responsible if a hidden problem turns up.

Quotes with a low total often leave out two or three of these lines. Quotes with a clear price before work starts let you decide with the whole picture. Our garbage disposal service page explains the repair and replace choice if you are not sure the old unit needs to go.

What goes in the quote, in cost drivers

What shapes the price.

  • The disposal unit itself. Horsepower, sound insulation and stainless parts raise the price before labor.
  • Electrical setup. A cord and outlet are simple. Hardwiring, a new switch or new circuit adds time or a second trade.
  • Dishwasher connection. The hose clamp and knockout plug add a small task, and a cracked hose adds a part.
  • Drain alignment and parts. A different height or worn fittings under the sink mean cutting and replacing pipe.
  • Access and cleanup. A cramped cabinet or a leaking base takes longer. Removing the old unit is part of the work.

Insurance sees a worn-out disposal as ordinary replacement, so expect no help there. Water damage from a sudden leak might be covered, so read your policy.

Doing it yourself in Eagle, ID: the DOPL permit rule

In Ada County and across the state, Idaho plumbing permits are issued by the state Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses, known as DOPL, not by the city. Homeowners may buy a plumbing permit for work on their own primary or secondary residence in Eagle, ID. That does not apply to commercial buildings.

A city or county building permit does not replace the DOPL permit. DOPL permits expire 365 days after purchase or last inspection, and inspections are requested through DOPL. Whether a simple disposal swap needs a permit depends on the work, so permit rules vary and you should ask DOPL at 208-334-3233.

If you hire a plumber, anyone can check the license with DOPL. Homes in Banbury Meadows were built in phases from 1994 to 2019, so under-sink layouts differ from house to house. The same goes for The Shores, where custom homes were built from 2006 to 2019. Measure under your own sink before you buy a unit.

Common questions

Why do garbage disposal installation costs vary so much?

The unit, the wiring, the dishwasher hookup and the drain fit all change. Two quotes can look far apart because one includes parts the other leaves out.

Does a bigger disposal cost more to install?

The unit costs more, and a heavier unit can need stronger mounting. The labor is similar unless wiring or drain pipe has to change.

Can I install a garbage disposal myself in Eagle, ID?

Homeowners may buy a plumbing permit through DOPL for work on their own primary or secondary residence. Ask DOPL whether your job needs one. If wiring is needed, use a licensed electrician.

Do I need an electrician for a new disposal?

Only if there is no outlet, switch or circuit to use. If a switch and outlet exist, plumbing alone covers it.

What if my sink is slow after the new disposal goes in?

The drain line may be partly blocked. A disposal does not clear a clog downstream, so have the line cleaned.
