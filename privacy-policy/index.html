Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Eagle Express Plumbing in Eagle, ID collects when you call us or visit this website, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you call (208) 679-8357, we collect your phone number, address, a description of the problem and anything else you tell us. This site has no forms, so it does not collect personal details through the page.
