Star Sewer & Water District and what it means for a backup

Costs and permits for plumbing work in Star

Idaho plumbing permits come from the state Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses, not the city, and a city or county building permit does not replace one. Permit rules vary, so ask DOPL at 208-334-3233 about your job.
