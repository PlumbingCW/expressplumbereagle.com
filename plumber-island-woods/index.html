Plumber in Island Woods, Eagle, ID

Island Woods is a neighborhood of custom homes in Eagle, ID, between the north and south channels of the Boise River, with its entrance off Eagle Road. Owners there can call Eagle Express Plumbing at (208) 679-8357.

Listing sources date Island Woods earlier than most of its neighbors. This page explains what age means for plumbing decisions in Ada County, without guessing about any one house.

What public sources say about Island Woods

One listing source says development began in 1993 and custom homes were built from 1993 through 2006, on lots of 0.22 to 0.54 acre. It reports a recreation center, a pool with clubhouse, tennis courts, a clock tower, a park, walking paths, private ponds and gated access.

The same source says a footbridge links the neighborhood to the Greenbelt and downtown Eagle. It places Hidden Island to the north and Two Rivers across Eagle Road to the west.

Treat these as listing claims.

The build years and amenities above were written by a real estate site, not by us or the county. Ask your HOA before you plan around any of them.

Homes from 1993 to 2006 are old enough to ask about

The Department of Energy puts storage tank life at 10 to 15 years. Even a heater installed in 2006 reached 15 years in 2021, so an original heater here is past the usual range. Most owners have probably replaced theirs, and the replacement has its own age to check.

Pipe material is a different question. Homes from this period can have copper, CPVC or early PEX, and we cannot know which you have until we see it. A leak does not mean the whole house needs new pipe, so we look for the failed section first.

Mature yards, ponds and the main line

Private ponds and a riverside setting are reported for this neighborhood. Trees and landscaping that have grown for years can send roots toward a sewer lateral, though we do not know the condition of any lateral here. A camera look is the way to find out.

The Eagle Sewer District serves sewer in Eagle, ID, and who owns the lateral from the house to the main depends on where the connection sits. Ask the district before any repair.

Utilities and permits to confirm

Common questions

Do you serve Island Woods in Eagle, ID?

Island Woods calls are welcome. Listing sources use ZIP 83616, and we check the street when you phone (208) 679-8357.

Is my Island Woods water heater too old?

Read the data plate. The Department of Energy says storage tanks last 10 to 15 years, and homes built before 2006 have had time to pass that range.

Does an older home mean I need to repipe?

No. One leak points to one failed section. Repeated leaks in different places are the sign to ask about repiping.

How do I find out who owns the sewer lateral?

Ask the Eagle Sewer District, since ownership depends on where the connection sits. We check the pipe and tell you what we find.

Can you inspect a drain line before I buy a home here?

A camera inspection shows the inside of the line at that moment. It does not predict future problems, and we say so.

Standards and sources

Plumbing work in Idaho follows the state plumbing code, which is based on the 2015 Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) with state amendments in IDAPA 24.39.20. Permits and licenses come from the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses (DOPL), not the city.
