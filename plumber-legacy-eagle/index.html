Plumber in Legacy, Eagle, ID

Legacy is a large master-planned neighborhood in Eagle, ID, between Linder Road and Highway 16, reached from Floating Feather Road. Homeowners there can reach Eagle Express Plumbing at (208) 679-8357.

Homes in Legacy went up over many years, so two neighbors can have very different plumbing. This page uses listing details to explain how that changes the questions we ask in Ada County.

What public sources say about Legacy

A listing source describes Legacy as 590 acres with 90 acres of common area, a community pool and clubhouse, three tennis courts, a 24-acre three-hole practice golf course and playgrounds. It also reports ponds, including a 7-acre pond, and lots from 0.08 to just over 2 acres.

That source says development began in 2006, the first homes were built in 2008 and 2009, and the project sat dormant until about 2012. Sample listings run from 2015 to 2024. Syringa and Homestead are named to the north.

About the figures above.

Acreage, amenities and dates for Legacy come from one listing page and were not verified against city records. Use them as background, not as a survey.

Several build eras on one street

A neighborhood built across 15 years or more has water heaters of very different ages. A unit from 2009 is past the Department of Energy range of 10 to 15 years, while one from 2022 is new. We read the label rather than assume.

Newer homes may have a tankless heater instead of a tank, which is a different kind of service call. Our tankless water heater page explains sizing and descaling.

Lot sizes from 0.08 acre to over 2 acres also mean very different yard line lengths.

Standards and sources

Plumbing work in Idaho follows the state plumbing code, which is based on the 2015 Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) with state amendments in IDAPA 24.39.20. Permits and licenses come from the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses (DOPL), not the city.
