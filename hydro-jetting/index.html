Why the snake came back: jetter versus cable

A drain cable, also called a snake, is a spinning steel cord with a cutting head. It is quick and works well on a simple clog near a fixture. The trouble is that it only cuts a hole the size of the cable, and the rest of the grease or scale stays on the pipe wall.

Cable machine and hydro jetter compared.
Point. Cable (snake). Hydro jetter.
How it clears. Cuts or pulls through the blockage. Scours the pipe wall with pressurized water.
Best for. A single clog near one fixture. Grease, soap scum, scale and roots in longer runs.
Leaves behind. A coating on the pipe wall. A clean pipe, if the pipe is sound.
Risk with weak pipe. Cable can snag a joint. High pressure can worsen a cracked or collapsed pipe.
Needs. Fixture or cleanout access. A cleanout or removed fixture, plus a way to catch the runoff.

For a single sink clog, we often start with a cable, and our drain cleaning page covers that work. If the same drain clogged before, we move to the jetter because the repeat tells us the walls are coated.

Pressure, hoses and nozzle types

A jetter is a pump, a long reel of hose and a nozzle. The pump pushes water out through small holes in the nozzle. The holes face backward, so the water drives the hose forward and flushes debris back toward the cleanout.

  • Penetrating nozzle: a narrow forward jet that opens a hard blockage so water can start moving.
  • Rotating or spinner nozzle: spins as it pulls back and scrubs the pipe wall in a full circle.
  • Root-cutting nozzle: a sharper, wider pattern that clears root masses at a joint.
  • Smaller hose and nozzle sizes for kitchen lines, larger ones for the main.

We match the nozzle and the pressure to the pipe, never the other way around. Old clay, thin cast iron and cracked plastic all need a gentler approach, and some pipe is not a candidate for jetting at all.

Camera first, every time

Before any jetting in Eagle, ID, we run a drain camera down the line when access allows. The monitor shows grease, roots, scale, a dip where water pools and any break in the pipe. After jetting, a second pass confirms the walls are clear.

Hydro jetting cannot fix a collapsed pipe, a joint knocked out of line, or a belly where the pipe sags. If the camera finds one, we say so and you decide between spot repair, lining or replacement. What drives the price of a hydro jetting visit

What shapes the price.

  • Access to the line. A clean outdoor cleanout makes the job faster. When we must pull a toilet or remove a fixture to get in, the visit takes longer.
  • Length and size of the line. A short kitchen run is quicker than a full main to the street. Larger pipe also takes more water and more time.
  • What is in the pipe. Light grease clears fast. Heavy scale, packed roots or years of buildup need several passes.
  • Camera work before and after. A camera run adds time, and it also keeps us from jetting a pipe that is already broken.
  • After-hours or same-day timing. A night or weekend call can cost differently than a scheduled visit, and we explain that before work starts.

Common questions

Do you handle hydro jetting emergencies at night or on weekends in Eagle?

Yes. A drain that backs up late at night does not have to wait for morning, since dispatch at (208) 679-8357 runs 24/7. Same-day appointments are offered when available. No arrival time is promised, so ask for the plan for tonight and avoid running more water into the line.

Will hydro jetting damage my pipes?

On sound pipe, no. On cracked, collapsed or very thin old pipe, high pressure can do harm, which is why we run a camera first and match the nozzle and pressure to what the pipe can take.

Why does my kitchen sink clog every few weeks after it was snaked?

A cable cuts a channel and leaves grease on the walls. The coating builds back until water stops again. Jetting removes the coating, and watching what goes down the drain keeps it from returning.

Who is responsible when the sewer main is blocked in Eagle, ID, me or the district?

The Eagle Sewer District runs the public main in Eagle, and the pipe on your lot is generally yours. The district can say where its part ends. If our camera shows the block is in the street main, we tell you and the district takes it from there.

Does hydro jetting get rid of tree roots for good?

It cuts roots out of the line, but roots return if a gap remains at the joint. When the camera shows the same joint filling again, a repair is the lasting fix.

Can a landlord or property manager schedule jetting for a tenant's drain?

Yes. Call the dispatch line with the property address and which drain is affected, and tell us if the tenant should be home for access.
