Attached homes and the question of who owns the pipe

In a building with shared walls, a leak can start in one unit and show up in another. The first step is stopping the water, which means knowing where your own shutoff is and whether there is a separate one for the building.

Which lines belong to you and which belong to the association is set by your HOA documents, not by us. We can tell you where a leak is. The documents tell you who pays and who is allowed to open the wall.
