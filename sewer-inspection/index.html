Sewer Camera Inspection in Eagle, ID

A sewer inspection is a camera run through the pipe that carries waste from your house to the public main. In Eagle, ID, people book one before buying a home, after repeat clogs, or before putting in landscaping over the line. The camera shows what is inside the pipe now, so you can decide with facts instead of guesses.

What a sewer camera shows you

A small waterproof camera head on a flexible push cable goes in through the cleanout and travels down the line. A light on the head and a monitor let us watch the pipe wall as it passes. A transmitter in the head lets a locator find the head from above ground, so we can mark where a problem sits and how deep it is.

  • Cracks, broken sections and offset joints.
  • Roots entering at joints.
  • Sags, called bellies, where water and waste pool.
  • Scale, grease and debris buildup.
  • Pipe material, such as plastic, clay or cast iron.
  • Connections from other lines, such as a second bathroom group.

Local fact.

The Eagle Sewer District provides sewer service in Eagle, ID, and the public main is its pipe. Our camera covers the private lateral on your lot, and we will tell you if the footage shows trouble in the street main.

We describe what we see in plain words and point to it on the screen. If you are buying, you can ask the plumber to walk the pipe with your agent on the line.

What the camera cannot tell you

A camera is a visual tool, and it has limits. It cannot see through a full blockage, so we may need to clear the line first. It does not pressure test the pipe, and it cannot see the public main unless the head reaches it from your cleanout.

A camera also does not replace a home inspector's report on the rest of the house. If you have a septic system instead of a sewer connection, tank condition is a separate trade. We do not guess at what we cannot see, and we will say when the footage is incomplete.

What drives the price of a sewer inspection

What shapes the price.

  • Access to the line. A usable cleanout speeds the job. If we must pull a toilet, it takes more time.
  • Length of pipe to scan. A short lateral takes less time than a long run to the street.
  • Cleaning before the scan. A blocked or heavily coated pipe may need clearing first, which adds a step.
  • Locating and marking. Marking the route and depth with a locator adds time but gives you a map of the pipe.
  • Written findings. If you need a summary of the findings for a lender or an agent, tell us when you call.

A camera inspection is a planned cost, not an insured loss. If the camera finds a defect, ask your agent how your policy treats that kind of damage before you file anything, since wear and gradual leaks are commonly excluded.

Common questions

Should I get a sewer inspection when buying a home in Eagle, ID?

It is a smart step, because the lateral is buried and a home inspector cannot see it. A camera shows cracks, roots and sags before you close. Ask early, since inspection windows are short.

Does a sewer camera inspection find where the pipe runs?

Yes. A transmitter in the camera head lets a locator mark the pipe path and depth on the ground. That helps you avoid it when digging or landscaping.

Can you do a camera inspection of a p-trap or a single fixture drain?

Sometimes. A small camera can pass some p-traps and short branch lines, but tight bends can stop it. We will tell you if a fixture drain is too tight to scope.

Which sewer district serves my home, and who maintains the main?

The Eagle Sewer District provides sewer service in Eagle, ID, but districts differ by address. Check which one serves yours and ask where its responsibility ends. The private lateral on your lot is the part we scope.

Do landlords and property managers schedule inspections too?

Yes. Send the address and cleanout details when you call, and tell us if a tenant needs to be home.

What if the camera cannot get through the line?

A full blockage or collapsed section can stop it. We then clear what we can, mark where the camera stopped and explain the options.
