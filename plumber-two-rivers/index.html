What public sources say about Two Rivers

Listing sites describe Two Rivers as mostly custom-built homes, generally built from 2000 through 2017, on lots from about 0.2 acre to more than an acre. They report a community pool, a clubhouse, a park, paved paths and pressurized irrigation.

They also report ponds, waterfalls and two gated phases called The Pointe and Heron Pointe. Mace River Ranch sits to the north.

Where these facts come from.

Every detail above comes from a public real estate listing page. Plat records and your HOA documents are the final word, so check them before relying on a number.

Why a custom home needs a look before a quote

Two houses on the same Two Rivers street can have different pipe layouts and water heater locations, so we do not assume a layout. We check the main shutoff, the heater and the first run of pipe before we say what a repair involves.

Listings show home sizes from about 2,000 square feet to estates over 8,000. Bigger houses usually have more bathrooms and longer hot water runs. That is general plumbing knowledge and not a fact about your address.

Irrigation leak or house leak in Two Rivers

Pressurized irrigation means a second pipe network runs through the yard beside the house water line. A soggy lawn or a high bill can come from either one.

The U.S. EPA says household leaks waste more than 9,300 gallons a year on average. Its meter test works here: read the meter, use no water for two hours, then read it again. If the meter moves, see our leak detection page.

Before anyone digs.

Call 811 before digging for any reason in Eagle, ID. Underground lines in a yard can include irrigation, water, gas and power.
