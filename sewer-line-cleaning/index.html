Emergency sewer line cleaning when the main backs up into the lowest drain

Waste water always finds the lowest opening, so a blocked main shows up at the basement floor drain, the ground-level shower or the toilet closest to the pipe. A single slow sink is usually a branch clog that drain cleaning can handle. Several fixtures gurgling or backing up together point to the main.

Weekend and after-hours calls go to the same 24/7 line, and we book same-day appointments when available. We never promise an arrival time, and we tell you plainly what we can do on the phone. Cutting off the water you add is the best thing you can do before a plumber shows up in Eagle, ID.

While you wait for us.

Stop using water. That means no flushing, no laundry and no dishwasher until the line is clear. Keep people and pets out of any room with sewage. Do not use chemical cleaner in the main, because it will sit in standing water and can burn whoever works on it next.

Find the cleanout before the plumber arrives

The cleanout is a capped pipe that gives a straight shot into the main. It is usually outside near the foundation, in a basement or in a utility room. Knowing where it is saves time.

  1. Walk the outside of the house near where the main line leaves, and look for a capped pipe at ground level.
  2. Check the basement or utility room if you have one, and the garage wall.
  3. Do not open the cap on a backed-up line. Water in the pipe can rush out when the cap loosens.
  4. Tell the dispatcher what you found, or that you could not find one.

If there is no cleanout, we can often reach the main by pulling a toilet. A missing cleanout is also worth fixing, and adding one while the line is open makes future cleanings easier.

Cable or jetter: how we clear a main

A cable machine feeds a rotating steel cable tipped with a cutting head through the blockage. It handles paper, soft clogs and some roots. A hydro jetter uses pressurized water to scour the pipe wall and works best on grease and heavy buildup.

Main line cleaning tools and when we choose each.
Tool. Clears. Watch for.
Cable with cutting head. Paper, soft clogs, thin roots. Leaves a coating on the pipe wall.
Hydro jetter. Grease, scale, packed roots. Not for collapsed or cracked pipe.
Drain camera. Nothing, it only looks. Cannot pass a full blockage.

We camera the line after cleaning to confirm it is clear and to check the pipe. See our hydro jetting page for how the high-pressure method works in detail.

Cleaning versus repair: when a clean line is not the fix

A cleaned main that backs up again within weeks is a message from the pipe. It often means roots at a joint, a sag where waste settles or a crack that lets soil in. The camera tells us which.

If it is a defect, cleaning only buys time. Our sewer line repair page explains spot repair, lining and replacement. We will not sell you a repair on a pipe a cleaning can fix, and we will not keep cleaning a pipe that needs repair.

Storm drains, yard drains and driveway culverts

Sewer line cleaning is not only for the house drain. Yard drains, area drains and driveway culvert lines can fill with leaves, silt and gravel. These lines carry rain and runoff, not waste, and they should not connect to the sanitary sewer.

We clear them with a cable or a jetter from the nearest access point, and we camera the run to see where it goes. If a culvert crosses a public road such as State Street or Eagle Road, or a ditch, that part belongs to whoever manages the road, and we do not work in the public right of way. Landlords in Ada County, Garden City included, can send us the address and the blocked drain.

Boise River history, spring runoff and the Eagle Sewer District

Recurring Boise River flooding is part of the history of Eagle, ID, even with Arrowrock Dam and later dams upstream. After a big storm or during spring runoff, tell us if your backup started with the weather. Heavy flows can load the public system, though we cannot say from here that it did.

The Eagle Sewer District serves sewer customers in Eagle, ID. If your neighbors back up at the same time, the problem may be in the district's main, not your line, and the district should hear about it. Ask the district where its responsibility ends and check which district serves your address.

What drives the price of a sewer line cleaning

What shapes the price.

  • Access to the main. A good outdoor cleanout is fast to use. If we must pull a toilet, the visit takes longer.
  • Length and condition of the line. A short main is quicker than a long run to the street. Heavy buildup or packed roots need more passes.
  • Tool needed. A cable clears a simple blockage. A jetter handles grease and scale but takes more time and equipment.
  • Camera before and after. The camera shows the cause and confirms the result. It adds time but prevents guessing.
  • Timing of the call. A night or weekend call may be priced differently from a scheduled visit. We explain that before work starts.

Clearing a clogged main is typically treated as upkeep, not a covered loss. Damage from sewage coming up through a floor drain may need a separate backup endorsement, so look at your policy or ask your agent.

Common questions

Do you handle sewer line cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Eagle?

Yes. A main line that backs up into the lowest drain cannot be ignored overnight, and dispatch at (208) 679-8357 runs 24/7. Same-day slots exist when the schedule has one, but no arrival time is promised. Stop running water, from sinks, the washer and flushing, while you wait.

Why does my sewer back up into the tub or shower?

The tub drain is often the lowest point in the house. When the main is blocked downstream, waste water rises to the lowest open drain, so the tub shows it first.

Can I use chemical drain cleaner on a main line clog?

We advise against it. The chemicals sit in standing water, can burn skin and can damage some pipes. A plumber also has to work in that water afterward.

How often should a main sewer line be cleaned?

There is no one schedule. A camera inspection tells you the condition and the buildup rate. If the line clogs repeatedly, cleaning on a schedule or a repair is likely the real answer.

Who is responsible for a blocked sewer main in Eagle, ID, me or the Eagle Sewer District?

The district runs the public main and your lateral runs from the house to it. Ask the district where its responsibility ends. If neighbors back up too, call the district as well as us.

Can you clear a yard drain or driveway culvert line?

Yes for the private part of the line, using a cable or jetter. We do not work in the public right of way, and we will tell you if the blockage is beyond your property.
