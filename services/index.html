Common questions

Do you work on both homes and businesses in Eagle?

Yes. Most calls are homes, and we take small commercial service calls such as drains, leaks and restroom repairs. Large construction projects are outside what we do.

Can I book a plumbing service online?

No. We are a call-only business, so every request starts with a phone call to (208) 679-8357. That keeps a person involved from the first minute.

Do you offer same-day service?

Same-day appointments are offered when available. We do not promise an arrival time, and the dispatcher will tell you honestly how the day looks.

Are your plumbers licensed?

Our plumbers are licensed and insured. You can check any Idaho plumber's license with the state Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses at 208-334-3233.

Which services do you not offer?

We do not pump septic tanks, and we do not do foundation or waterproofing construction. We will say so on the call and suggest the right trade.

Standards and sources

Plumbing work in Idaho follows the state plumbing code, which is based on the 2015 Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) with state amendments in IDAPA 24.39.20. Permits and licenses come from the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses (DOPL), not the city.
