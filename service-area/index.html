Where we work

Our home base is Eagle, ZIP 83616, a city of about 30,000 people ten miles northwest of downtown Boise. From there we serve two neighboring cities with their own pages.

Eagle, ID 83616.

Our home base. State Street and Eagle Road are the main routes, and the Boise River Greenbelt runs through town.

Star, ID 83669.

West of Eagle along State Street. Star grew quickly in the 2010s, so newer homes and new plumbing are common.

Garden City, ID 83714.

Surrounded by Boise on the Boise River, with Chinden Boulevard as its main street and housing from several decades.

Nearby places we can usually reach

Meridian and Boise sit close to Eagle. We do not run separate pages for them, but you are welcome to call. Give the dispatcher the address and we will say whether a same-day appointment is possible. How we handle a call from outside Eagle

The process is the same in every town. A person answers, asks what is happening and where the water is, and helps you close the right valve. We then offer a same-day appointment when available and explain that we do not promise an arrival time.

A licensed plumber diagnoses the problem, gives a clear price before work starts and waits for your approval. If the job needs a state permit, the plumber explains it before any work begins.

Star and Garden City each have different water and sewer arrangements from Eagle, which shapes who owns a leak. The town pages explain the differences in more detail, and the dispatcher can confirm them for your address.

Who supplies utilities across our service area

Water and sewer providers change from town to town, and the provider decides where a leak is your problem and where it is theirs.

  • Eagle: Veolia Water Idaho, the City of Eagle or a private well for water, and the Eagle Sewer District for sewer.
  • Star: Star Sewer & Water District runs its own water delivery system.
  • Garden City: the City of Garden City provides water, sewer, trash and recycling.
  • Natural gas across the area: Intermountain Gas Company, with a 24-hour emergency line at 800-548-3679.

Landmarks that help us find you

In Eagle, mention Heritage Park downtown, the Boise River Greenbelt, State Street or Eagle Road. In Star, State Street and Westpointe Park work well. In Garden City, Chinden Boulevard, Expo Idaho and the Glenwood Street bridge are easy references. Your street address always comes first, but a landmark helps the dispatcher plan the route.

Standards and sources

Plumbing work in Idaho follows the state plumbing code, which is based on the 2015 Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) with state amendments in IDAPA 24.39.20. Permits and licenses come from the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses (DOPL), not the city.
